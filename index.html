About

Profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Science and Technology of China, jointly working with the High Magnetic Field Laboratory of the Hefei Institutes of Physical Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My doctoral research centers on asymmetric spin transport and the associated dynamics of antiferromagnets.

Before starting my Ph.D., I received my B.S. in Physics from Fudan University. Across my work, I am especially interested in how microscopic magnetic interactions shape transport behavior, switching mechanisms, and the motion of topological magnetic textures.
